# Backlink Plan

Real backlinks cannot be created from inside the codebase. They have to come from other websites linking to your tool pages. What you *can* do in the product is make the pages easier to link to and easier to discover. This repo now supports that with a fuller sitemap and stronger internal linking.

## Best pages to pitch first

- `/tool/ai-meta-generator`
- `/tool/keyword-clustering-tool`
- `/tool/schema-markup-generator`
- `/tool/regex-explainer`
- `/tool/instagram-caption-generator`

These pages have clear search intent, easy demos, and strong utility value, which makes them better outreach targets than generic category pages.

## Backlink sources to pursue

- AI tool roundups
- SEO tool list posts
- Developer resource pages
- Startup directories
- Newsletter curators
- Guest posts on marketing and dev blogs
- Community posts with a real tutorial or use case

## Linkable asset ideas

- "Free AI SEO tools" comparison page
- "Best free keyword clustering workflows" tutorial
- "Regex examples explained" reference post
- "SoftwareApplication schema examples" guide
- "Instagram caption formulas" blog post

## Anchor text suggestions

- AI meta generator
- keyword clustering tool
- schema markup generator
- regex explainer
- Instagram caption generator

Keep anchor text varied. Mix exact match with branded and natural anchors such as `Toolsy AI meta generator`, `free keyword clustering tool`, or `this regex explainer`.

## Outreach angle examples

- Show a before-and-after example from the tool
- Offer a tutorial that uses the tool, not just a homepage link
- Share a niche use case for ecommerce, SaaS, agencies, or creators
- Lead with utility and proof, not "please link to my site"

## What to avoid

- Buying spammy directory links
- Repeating the exact same anchor text everywhere
- Pointing all links only to the homepage
- Using thin tool pages with no explanation or examples
